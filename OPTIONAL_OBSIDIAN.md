# Optional guide to an Obsidian interview dashboard

Skip this guide if you already like your spreadsheet. The kit can find roles, prepare applications, record receipts and run scheduled searches with Obsidian turned off.

1. When you want a dashboard, [download Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/download) on your computer. If you use the Lite or Local edition, first set up the private workspace from the [Advanced guide](ADVANCED.md). The exporter needs access to the records on your computer.
2. Tell your assistant “Enable the optional Obsidian export for my job search. Show me the folder you created.” Choose the private job search workspace. A *vault* is simply a folder of notes that Obsidian can open.
3. In Obsidian select **Open folder as vault** and choose the exact generated **obsidian** folder. Open **Home**, then **Calendar**. An empty calendar is expected until you add a dated interview.
4. To check it, use a real invitation you choose to share. Confirm the employer, date, time and time zone. Ask the assistant to refresh, then open the calendar entry. If only invited so far, keep it under invitations until you book a time.
5. To stop, say “Turn off Obsidian exports and keep using my spreadsheet.” Your existing notes stay in the folder. You can enable exports again later.

The default board and calendar work without a subscription or community plugin. If you want a column board, enable Kanban through Obsidian’s community plugins. Phone sync is a separate choice. Keep personal notes in the designated notes area and change application stages through your tracker. [Export, sync and calendar details](references/obsidian.md)
